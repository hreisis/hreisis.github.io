Signal-Lab

Turn an observation into a rule you can inspect.

A TradingView indicator developed to translate price-action research into repeatable entry alerts, staged exits, and ATR-based risk controls. The work focuses on making each rule visible and comparing how different signal filters behave.

01

Define what a setup means

Research starts with consolidation, trend, volume, and entry confirmation. The rule sequence separates a watch condition from next-day confirmation and an entry. Markers are generated as the rules trigger; Replay shows that process unfolding through historical bars.

02

Plan the exit as well as the entry

Position management is part of the rule design: staged reductions, an ATR-based risk boundary, and a reclaim window make the response to changing price conditions explicit. The orange ATR line and entry, exit, and reclaim markers expose the rule state directly on the chart.

03

Compare, refine, repeat

Historical signal cohorts help compare volume, trend, and confirmation filters before freezing a rule version. MSTR is the current research example, with daily candidates and weekly observations reviewed separately. The recording below uses RDW to show the implemented indicator in motion; it is not a MSTR recording or a claim of validated performance.
